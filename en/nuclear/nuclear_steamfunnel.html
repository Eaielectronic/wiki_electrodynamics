Steam Funnel

Advertisement

Role

The steam funnel allows you to collect and redirect steam produced by reactors through gas pipes, when it is not possible to place turbines directly above the reactor.

Collecting Steam

Place the funnel in place of a turbine (where the steam rises). Then, connect a gas pipe to the top to transport the steam.

Releasing to a Turbine

To inject steam into a distant turbine:

  • Place a turbine on top of a funnel
  • Connect the gas pipe to the bottom of this funnel

Extremely Hot Steam

Steam in pipes does not cool down, but it is so hot that it is very difficult to store. Avoid long distances and unsuitable tanks.
