Atomic Assembler

Item Duplication

The Atomic Assembler exploits the properties of Dark Matter to duplicate any item. Items with internal inventories cannot be duplicated.

ParameterValue
Power Consumption72 kW @ 480 V
Duplication Duration6,000 ticks (5 minutes)
Uses per Cell12 uses

Usage: Place the item you wish to duplicate into the assembler, then surround it with Dark Matter Cells.

Progress Loss

If the assembler loses power during the duplication process, all progress is lost. Ensure a stable power supply.

Teleporter

The teleporter transports an entity from one point to another. **Two teleporters** are required: a transmitter and a receiver. Only the transmitter needs to be powered.

ParameterValue
Energy per Teleport5 MJ
Recharge Delay4 seconds
Two-way TravelRequires 2 teleporters

Linking: Enter the destination coordinates directly into the interface, or use a Frequency Card to import coordinates via the Import button.

Frequency Card

Right-click the card on a block to store its coordinates. Shift + Right-click to clear. The card can also store the dimension — perfect for inter-dimensional travel.

Chunk Loader

The chunk loader keeps a 3×3 chunk area (centered on its own chunk) always loaded. It requires no energy to operate.

Utility

Indispensable for keeping your reactors active when you move away. Place one near every major installation.
