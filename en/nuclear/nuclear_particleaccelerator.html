Particle Accelerator

Advertisement

Advanced Construction

The accelerator is one of the most complex structures in the mod. It produces Antimatter and Dark Matter, which are required for end-game machinery. Moving particles emit radiation.

Principle — Collision Results

Two particles collide. The result depends on their combined speed according to the following formula:

result = ((v1 + v2) / 4) ²
ResultConditionGuarantee
Dark Matterresult > 0.999 (both particles at ≥ 99.99% C)100%
Antimatterresult < 0.999 (both particles at ≥ 50% C)Variable — increases with speed
NothingOne particle at less than 50% C—

Method 1 — Straight Line

The simplest method: a long line of boosters to reach the desired speed. It takes 200 boosters in a straight line to reach 100% C. Bulky but effective.

Minimize Turns

Each turn costs −0.17%/tick in speed. Keep straight lines as long as possible and limit corners to a strict minimum.

Collision Ring (minimum 3×3)

Particles expire after 1,000 ticks. Therefore, the ring must be as small as possible. A 3×3 ring of electromagnets is sufficient.

Method 2 — Cyclotron (Compact)

More complex but space-efficient. Additionally requires:

  • Electromagnetic Gateway — lets particles pass above a programmable speed threshold.
  • Electromagnetic Diode — forces circulation direction (the arrow must point in the direction of movement).

Select Method in Injector

The injector must be configured for the method used via the tab in its interface. You can also view the speed of both particles by hovering over the Speed tab.
