Nuclear Control

By now, you have probably noticed that interacting manually with reactors and their various supporting equipment is incredibly tedious. On top of that, you have to expose yourself to deadly radiation in order to refuel the Fission and MS reactors. Surly there has to be some way to automate things to relieve the burden you might be saying. Well say no more, because that's exactly what the Reactor Logistics Network is for! The RL Network will allow you to accomplish all of these things and more. The following pages will cover the components of the network.
