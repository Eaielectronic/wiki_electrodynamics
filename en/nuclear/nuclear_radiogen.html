Radioisotope Generator

Advertisement

Reactor Alternative

The Radioisotope Generator produces electricity from the radioactive decay heat of materials. No steam, no turbines — simple and compact.

Usage

Simply place a radioactive item into the generator. It immediately begins producing power. No configuration required.

Power Production

The power output depends on two factors:

  • Radioactivity level of the item — the more radioactive it is, the more power it produces
  • Quantity of items — stacking multiple items multiplies the production

Compatible Items

Any radioactive item works. Check the Radiation page to see which items emit radiation. The most efficient ones are:

Great Start
