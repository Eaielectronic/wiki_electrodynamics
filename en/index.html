Nuclear Science & Electrodynamics Wiki

Welcome to the wiki for Nuclear Science and Electrodynamics mods for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge). Use the left panel to navigate chapters.

How to use this wiki

Each page matches a chapter from the in-game guidebook. Both mods are covered in English and French.

Warning — Radiation

Handling radioactive materials without Hazmat equipment will inflict radiation damage. Always wear a full Hazmat suit before touching uranium or plutonium.
